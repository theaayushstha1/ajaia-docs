Weekly sync — engineering

Attendees: Ada, Grace, Alan

Decisions

We ship the import path before comments. A document that arrives broken is
never trusted again, and comments on a broken document are worse than no
comments at all.

Sharing stays owner-only. Collaborators can edit the body and nothing else.
Passing access along is a separate feature with a separate conversation.

Open questions

Do we snapshot a version on every save, or only on demand? Every save is
honest but expensive; on demand is cheap but forgets the moment that
mattered.

What happens to a shared document when its owner leaves the company?
